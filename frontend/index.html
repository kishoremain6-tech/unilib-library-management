Library rules

3-day loans

Due date is the borrow date plus 3 calendar days. Returning on the due date is on time.

₹10 per day

A fine starts the day after the due date and grows ₹10 for each overdue day. The server calculates it.

Up to 5 books

Return overdue books before borrowing again.

Librarian help

Fines can be marked paid or waived by the librarian, with a recorded reason.
